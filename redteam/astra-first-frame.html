How the shared work is counted

Fabricated ductwork and pipe supports sold by Mechanical Systems and Cooling engineers are reported on their home sheet and again on the Fabrication sheet. Adding the sheet totals would count that revenue twice. Every total on this page counts each product line once, in its home vertical, and the Fabrication row shows only work sold by its own team.

Sheet sum 139,206 less shared revenue 4,201 = 135,005 netted YTD revenue.

Reconciliation note: the supplied P&L reports 135,007 YTD revenue. The sales table reports 135,005. These inherited figures are preserved here; their rounding bases require reconciliation.

Read the accounting basis

Gross margin
Revenue less the cost of goods sold, before any operating cost.
BU profitability
Gross margin less the four operating cost lines above. The vertical head is judged on this rung.
BU-level net profit
What the vertical keeps after every cost it carries, including group charges.
The inherited readout reports 74% of YTD net profit budget. Its comparator prorates the full-year budget by elapsed months. A phased YTD profit budget is not supplied. Do not treat that approximation as an approved monthly plan.
