## Design Context

### Users

The primary reader is a managing director reviewing the MIS in a live executive demo. He is commercially focused, not technically fluent, and must be able to understand revenue, profit, cost, collections, and team performance at a glance.

### Brand Personality

Decisive, credible, controlled. The interface should feel like a carefully prepared management instrument rather than a technical analytics tool.

### Aesthetic Direction

Editorial and information-dense, with strong hierarchy, restrained colour, precise typography, and both light and dark themes. Prefer plain business language and instantly readable numbers over dashboard jargon or excessive precision.

### Design Principles

- Lead with the commercial answer, then supporting evidence.
- Make units and comparisons understandable without prior finance vocabulary.
- Preserve exact source values while rounding presentation to useful precision.
- Keep dense tables and charts aligned, calm, and readable at desktop and phone widths.
- Use emphasis sparingly so important exceptions and management signals remain obvious.
